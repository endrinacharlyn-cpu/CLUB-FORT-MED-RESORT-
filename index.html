HISTORY OF THE RESORT

Club Fort Med Resort was founded in August 2005 by Mr. Marlon Medida. Before the resort was built, the area was filled with trees, and most of the land was surrounded by natural greenery. Mr. Medida purchased the property from his aunt at a lower price due to its geographic location. At first, the resort was intended only as a family business, since Mr. Medida was working in the corporate world at that time. The original purpose of building the resort was to serve as a retirement vacation home for his family.

FOUNDER OF THE CLUB FORT MED

Mr. Marlon Medida is the owner and founder of Club Fort Med Resort, which was established in August 2005. Before becoming a resort owner, he worked in the corporate world, where he gained experience in business and management. His vision was not only to build a business but also to create a peaceful place where families could relax and enjoy time together.

The land where the resort now stands was once full of trees and natural greenery. Mr. Medida purchased the property from his aunt at a lower price due to its geographic location. At first, the resort was built for family use only and was intended to become a retirement vacation home. However, as time passed, the resort began to grow and attract more visitors, which led to its development into a public resort.

Through his dedication and hard work, Mr. Marlon Medida successfully transformed a simple vacation home into what is now known as Club Fort Med Resort, a place enjoyed by many guests today.

THE SUCCESS STORY OF CLUB FORT MED

Before Club Fort Med Resort became successful, it went through many stages of growth and development. At first, the resort only had two to three rooms. As time passed and the owner earned enough income, he slowly invested his money to add more rooms and improve the facilities.

The resort became widely known during the rise of Oslob’s famous tourist attraction, the Butanding (whale shark watching). At that time, Oslob still had very few resorts, which made Club Fort Med one of the top choices for tourists looking for a place to stay. Because of this opportunity, many visitors stayed at the resort, which greatly increased its popularity.

Aside from catering to tourists, the resort also served different LGUs (Local Government Units) and even offered a payment option where clients could pay their balance through monthly installments. The income earned from tourists and clients was carefully reinvested into the business to expand the resort by adding more rooms, new attractions, and facility upgrades—especially the swimming pools—to meet the growing demands of the market.

Today, Club Fort Med Resort is now well-known and successful, proving that through hard work, wise investments, and dedication, a small family vacation home can grow into a thriving resort business.

CHALLENGES MET BY THE FOUNDER

Like any growing business, the founder of Club Fort Med Resort faced several challenges during the early years of operation. One of the biggest struggles was financial limitation, since the resort started as a small business and required careful budgeting to slowly expand and improve. The owner had to wisely manage the money needed to keep the business running while also saving enough funds to reinvest in its development.

Another major challenge was keeping up with the changing demands of the market. As more tourists began to visit and competition increased, the founder needed to ensure that the resort continued to improve its facilities and services.
